FrameFuse Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 27, 2026

This policy explains how the FrameFuse Android app ("FrameFuse", "the app", "we") handles your information. FrameFuse is a camera app that records a video or photo once and produces both a 9:16 and a 16:9 version of it.

Summary

  • FrameFuse does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal data.
  • Everything you record stays on your device.
  • No account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • The app does not connect to the internet.

Permissions the app uses

  • Camera – to show the live preview and record videos and photos.
  • Microphone – to record sound with your videos. You can turn sound off in the app.
  • Storage (Android 9 and older only) – to save exported videos and photos to your gallery. On Android 10 and newer no storage permission is needed.

Camera and microphone data are processed only on your device while you use the app. They are never uploaded or sent to us or to anyone else.

Data stored on your device

  • Your recordings and photos, plus small thumbnails, kept in the app's private storage until you delete them.
  • Exported files that you choose to save, placed in your device gallery (Movies/FrameFuse and Pictures/FrameFuse).
  • Project and take details (names, trim points, favourites) and app settings (for example resolution and frame rate).

You can delete takes and projects inside the app at any time. Uninstalling FrameFuse removes all of its private data. Files you exported to your gallery remain until you delete them yourself.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share data with any third party. When you use the Share button, your device's standard Android share menu opens and the file goes only to the app you pick. That app's own privacy policy then applies.

Children

FrameFuse is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Security

Because your content never leaves your device through FrameFuse, its security depends on your device's own protections, such as your screen lock and encryption.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
